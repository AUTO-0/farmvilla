BEFORE YOUR STORY BEGINS

Good to know.

We are preparing our very first villa. Here are the details that matter before you make an enquiry.

Is FARMVILLA open for stays?

Villa 01 is in the pre-launch stage. We have not announced an opening date. Booking requests are not confirmed stays until our team approves them.

What is planned for Villa 01?

A one-bedroom farm villa with a private bathroom, compact kitchenette, wardrobe space and outdoor sitting area. Final amenities will be confirmed before opening.

Are the rates final?

Not yet. Published figures are indicative. Any agreed amount will be confirmed by the owner before the reservation is approved.

How do booking requests work?

Choose dates using the live calendar, submit guest details and receive a reference. Requested dates are temporarily held, and the villa team reviews the booking. A reference does not guarantee a confirmed stay.

Can I book parties or private pools?

Small occasions may be considered individually and are subject to approval. Private pools and additional villas are future ideas, not current facilities.
