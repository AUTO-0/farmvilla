GUEST INFORMATION · PRE-LAUNCH

Privacy & booking requests.

We request only the information necessary to manage a FARMVILLA booking enquiry: your name, phone number, optional email, requested stay dates, guest count and message.

How your information is used

Authorized staff use these details to assess availability, contact you about the requested stay, provide a quote and manage confirmation, cancellations or your invoice. A submitted request is not a confirmed reservation. No online payment-card information is collected.

Where booking data is held

Booking records are stored in a secured Supabase-backed booking database with access restricted to authorized villa administrators. The public website can request available dates but cannot read the private customer booking table.

Your choices

Do not include identity-document numbers, bank card details or other sensitive information in your message. You may ask the villa operator to correct or remove an enquiry through the official contact channel provided when they respond to your reservation. Legal and accounting retention obligations may apply to completed transactions.

Feedback and publicity

We do not automatically publish your booking details. Guest reviews are published only after moderation and consent; they are not part of the booking form.

Before you book

Rates, policies and amenities are subject to owner confirmation. A booking reference confirms receipt of a request only; staff approval is required to confirm your stay. Dates may be held for up to 24 hours while the request is reviewed.
